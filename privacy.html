Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Your privacy

Nevercam does not collect or send your personal data to the developer. You do not need an account. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Photos and videos

Your camera images and microphone audio are processed on your device. Captures are saved to your photo library. Nevercam does not upload them to developer servers. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple may sync them according to your settings.

Local storage

The app stores settings, presets and a list of its captures on your device. A completed video that could not be saved to Photos is kept locally so you can retry. Temporary files may be created when you share a capture. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Permissions

Camera access is used to take photos and videos. Microphone access adds sound to videos. Photos access lets you save captures and view or delete them in the app’s library. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings.

Optional location

Location is off by default. If you turn it on and grant permission, your location may be included in the metadata of new photos and videos. It is not sent to the developer. Turning it off does not remove location already saved in existing captures.

Sharing

Captures and presets are shared only when you choose to share them. The apps or services you select handle the shared content under their own privacy policies. Shared files may include metadata such as location if you enabled it.

Deleting data

You can delete captures in Nevercam or Photos. Deleting a capture in Nevercam also deletes the original from Photos after your confirmation. Photos may keep deleted items in Recently Deleted. Deleting Nevercam from your device removes its local app data; captures saved in Photos remain. Backup and iCloud retention are controlled by your Apple settings.

Apple services

Apple may handle backups, iCloud Photos and system diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy policy. These services are separate from data collection by Nevercam.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app’s privacy practices change. For privacy questions, email nevercam.app@gmail.com.
